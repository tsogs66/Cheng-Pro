# e-ORB audit — what was checked, what was found, what is still open

Voyage Chief / ChEng AIO `eorb.js`. Updated 14 Nov 2026 after a research pass on the Cheng Marine Suite environment. The full findings, each marked VERIFIED or UNVERIFIED with its link, are in [`docs/eorb-flag-research/`](eorb-flag-research/README.md).

## Checked and fixed in the program

| Area | Result |
|---|---|
| Every entry type × every flag | `tests/test_eorb_flags_sweep.js` builds every item and scenario for all 10 flags (1,935 combinations): nothing throws, every entry produces record lines, no `undefined`/`NaN`, signatory rules follow each flag's policy. It found that Part II Code D (crude oil washing) items 13 and 14 were run through the Part I bilge wording and threw. Fixed. |
| Appendix III, Part I | Compared with the official form (Hong Kong and Liberian reproductions): every code letter A–I and item number matches. |
| Appendix III, Part II | Items 1–84 match (RMI MI-402A confirms 85 and the R 84 wording). **Added** the missing item **85** (R: date, signature and stamp of port authority official); **R 84** now reads "Total quantity of ballast water discharged"; titles of F, J and L and items 37 and 50 follow the official wording. |
| Part III | Part III is **not** an IMO form. Only Panama's book has one (MMC-215, 6 Mar 2026). Its official Part III form was supplied and is now followed item by item: **A** ODS (1, 1.1, 1.4, 1.5), **B** NOx Tier III engine status (2), **C** SOx bunker data (3) and low-sulphur changeover volumes (4), **D** vapour collection, tankers (5), **E** incinerator (6, 7, 8). It is offered, supplemented and followed on **only for flags that require it** (`partIII: true`, Panama). Other flags get Parts I and II; Part III entries already saved stay in the book. |
| Burning of waste | Sludge in an incinerator: Code C 12.3; in a **boiler**: C 12.4 (Circ.736 example 7). Paper, wood, domestic garbage, **oily rags and used filters**: Garbage Record Book only (MEPC.1/Circ.736; Annex V Reg. 10). Panama MMC-215 para 5.6.6 also wants each incinerator use in ORB Part I and Part III, so the "Garbage incinerated" entry is offered on Panama-flag ships only. |
| Signatories | **Liberia** (Marine Operations Note 7/2010) and **Malta** (Technical Notice MARPOL.7): the officer in charge signs, the Master each page. They were set to "officer + Chief Engineer" (Liberia) and "Chief Engineer only" (Malta); both corrected. **Cyprus** "Chief Engineer only" could not be traced to any document (Circular 22/2020 states no signatory rule) and now follows the Convention default. **Marshall Islands** (documents supplied by the user: MG 2-13-3 Rev. Dec/2017, MI-402A Rev. Nov/2025, MI-332A Rev. 4/11): the officer in charge signs and dates each operation, the Chief Engineer / Chief Mate / SMS-named person *reviews* it, the Master countersigns each page — so it is no longer a two-signature-row flag. |
| Flag notes | Corrected: Liberia POL-012 is **Rev. 08/24**; RMI "MI-402A" is a real document (Rev. Nov/2025) — it was removed in the first pass because the research could not find it and is back;  RMI Declaration is issued on application (form MI-334A); Singapore: a Recognised Organisation approves the book and issues the Declaration; Hong Kong: English, or English and Chinese; Bahamas: MN056 added, MN010 is v2.3 of 2 Oct 2025. |
| Malta, Cyprus, Marshall Islands (documents supplied by the user) | **Malta Technical Notice MARPOL.11 Rev.1 (8 Aug 2023)** supersedes MARPOL.7 and MARPOL.11: e-RBs approved against MEPC.312(74) with a Declaration on board; it states no signatory rule, so Malta follows the MARPOL default. **Cyprus Circular 22/2020 (4 Nov 2020)**: ORB Parts I and II may be kept electronically; RO Declaration, then the Ministry's Declaration within 3 months (EUR 20); fuel changeover is a separate record, so no Part III; states no signatory rule. **RMI MN 2-013-3 (Rev. Mar/2017)**: inoperative equipment and the numbered sealing of overboard valves are documented in the ORB and other logs; receipts for shoreside discharges; the ORB is checked against the other logs. The app's OWS failure and valve sealing scenarios already cover this. |
| Flag guide | Entry Codes › Flag guide lists each flag's publications with a "Read" or "Listed only" tag, links to the originals and the history of changes (IMO, Liberia, Marshall Islands, Panama, Bahamas, Singapore, Malta, Hong Kong, Cyprus, UK), and a "which book records the burning of each kind of waste" table. A reference list with links, not a copy. |

## Still open

1. **UK** (MIN 644) and **Norway** (NMA) could not be read; Cyprus Circular 36/2010 is listed only. RMI MG 2-13-3, MI-402A, MI-332A and MN 2-013-3, Malta MARPOL.11 Rev.1 and Cyprus Circular 22/2020 were supplied by the user and read.
2. **Panama Part III.** The official form pages were supplied and read; the app now follows them (the earlier own-design changeover items C 1–4 and the Part III copies of OWS tests, seals, de-bunkering, receipts and lube were removed because the form has no such items). Items 1.2 and 1.3 of Code A do not appear on the specimen pages supplied, so they are not modelled.
3. **Other flags and Part III.** No document read for Liberia, Bahamas, Malta or Hong Kong has one. Marshall Islands, Singapore, Cyprus, UK and Norway are unknown; a flag is added with `partIII: true` once its circular says so.
4. **MEPC.1/Circ.736/Rev.2** itself was not read (the original circular was, via Liberia).
5. **Panama language** "English / Spanish" is unverified.
