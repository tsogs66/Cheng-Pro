Cheng Station

A proposed look for ChEng AIO, Voyage Chief and Tank Chief. Open a screen, then use the bar at the top to switch night / day and which product’s menu you are looking at.

  • Home — voyage, ROB, tanks, sync, e-ORB, and the rest of today’s summary boards
  • Noon report — log entry, one step at a time on a phone
  • Soundings — tank monitoring
  • ROB
  • Calibration
  • Vessel Setup

The direction is written up in README.md, including the menu map and where every current dashboard card went.
